句子 = 主语 + 谓语

## 1	名词

### 1.1	名词短语

限定词 + 修饰词 + 主体词

限定词

- 冠词 a an the
- 形容词性物主代词 my his
- 名词所有格 One's, Tom's
- 指示代词 this that
- 不定代词 some every another
- 基数词 one two
- 疑问代词 what which

限定词在可数名词复数 boys, 不可数名词 news, 专有名词 China 可以省略

在句意明显的时候, 主体词也可以省略 the rich (people), I want these (books)

### 1.2	可数名词与不可数名词

是否好数, 在它 "生下来的时候" 或 状态是否变化

不可数名词

- 不管
- 量化 a glass of water
- 可数化 a good time

### 1.3	代词

#### 1.3.1	人称代词

| **主格** | **宾格** | **形容词性物主代词** | **名词性物主代词** | **反身代词**   |
| ------ | ------ | ------------ | ----------- | ---------- |
| I      | me     | my           | mine        | myself     |
| You    | you    | your         | yours       | yourself   |
| He     | him    | his          | his         | himself    |
| She    | her    | her          | hers        | herself    |
| It     | it     | its          | its         | itself     |
| We     | us     | our          | ours        | ourselves  |
| You    | you    | your         | yours       | yourselves |
| They   | them   | their        | theirs      | themselves |

形容词性物主代词 + 名词 = 名词性物主代词   This book is my book = This book is mine

#### 1.3.2	指示代词

This, These 

That 还可以用于代指可数名词单数和不可数名词 

- The weather of ChengDu is coller of the weather of Chengdu.

Those 还可以用于代指可数名词复数

- The oranges of HuaiNan are sweeter than those of HuaiBei.

#### 1.3.3	不定代词

some, any, every, each, no

both, either, neither, all, none

many, much, more, most

little, a little, few, a few

other, others, another, the other, the others

half, several

复合不定代词:

(every, some, any, no) \* (thing, body)

#### 1.3.4	疑问代词

**Who** is he?

**What** do you want?

other + 可数名词复数 = others, other 不能单独用
